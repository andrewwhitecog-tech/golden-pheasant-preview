# Golden Pheasant business launch readiness

Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

This is the operator checklist for turning the current Golden Pheasant portfolio concept into an
Oregon painting contractor business. It is operational planning, not legal advice. Recheck every
government requirement at the linked official source before paying, filing, advertising, bidding,
or accepting construction work.

## Current verified checkpoint

- The public site is a noindex portfolio concept, not a painting-contractor advertisement.
- Andrew confirmed on 2026-08-01 that Golden Pheasant did not yet have a CCB license.
- A 2026-09-01 exact-name search of the Oregon CCB database returned zero records. The CCB warns
  that a name-search miss is not proof that no license exists, so the site must stay in concept mode
  until an active number is supplied and independently matched to the business.
- Oregon CCB currently lists painting, handyman work, and most construction or repair services as
  license-required work.
- Oregon CCB currently lists gutter cleaning, pressure/power washing solely for cleaning, and debris
  cleanup as examples that do not require a CCB license. Keep those scopes strictly cleaning-only;
  do not bundle repair, coating, sealing, staining, installation, or improvement work into them.
- `goldenpheasantpc.com` had no DNS record and returned no Verisign RDAP record on 2026-09-01.
  That is a moment-in-time check, not a reservation.

## Launch sequence

### 1. Owner decisions before any paid action

- [ ] Confirm the public business name and whether it will be a sole proprietorship, LLC, or other
  entity with a qualified adviser.
- [ ] Choose the residential/commercial endorsement that matches the intended jobs.
- [ ] Decide whether Golden Pheasant will employ anyone at launch.
- [ ] Approve a startup budget for training, exam, registration, bond, liability insurance,
  application, equipment, and domain/email.
- [ ] Confirm the intended service area, phone, email, and privacy-safe business address.

### 2. Oregon contractor-license lane

The Oregon CCB's current nine-step path should control this work:

- [ ] Select the Responsible Managing Individual (RMI).
- [ ] Complete at least 16 hours of approved pre-license training and pass the Oregon exam.
- [ ] Select the correct endorsement and business structure.
- [ ] Register the entity and/or assumed business name with the Oregon Secretary of State.
- [ ] Obtain the required CCB surety bond.
- [ ] Obtain general liability insurance naming the CCB as certificate holder.
- [ ] Obtain workers' compensation coverage if employees will be hired, plus any required employer
  and tax account numbers.
- [ ] Submit a complete CCB application and supporting documents.
- [ ] Wait for an active CCB record; independently verify the exact business name, endorsement,
  status, bond, insurance, and number before changing the site.

Current official figures shown by the CCB on 2026-09-01 are a $60 exam fee and a $400 initial
application fee. Training, bond, insurance, entity-registration, equipment, and specialty-license
costs are separate and must be quoted before purchase.

### 3. Lead-safe painting gate

- [ ] Before bidding on or disturbing painted surfaces in pre-1978 homes or child-occupied
  facilities, confirm whether the job triggers Oregon lead-based-paint requirements.
- [ ] If applicable, complete the approved eight-hour RRP course and obtain the required firm and
  Oregon lead-based-paint licensing tied to the active CCB license.
- [ ] Put the lead-safe status on the site only after the credential and scope are verified.

### 4. Operating system before first painting lead

- [ ] Define written estimating, change-order, deposit, cancellation, warranty, and closeout rules.
- [ ] Build a job-cost template covering labor, prep, coatings, consumables, travel, waste, overhead,
  taxes, and contingency. Do not revive the public ballpark estimator until its ranges are approved.
- [ ] Create scope boundaries for prep, drywall repair, carpentry, pressure washing, lead-safe work,
  customer-supplied paint, access, furniture movement, cleanup, and exclusions.
- [ ] Prepare safety data sheets, PPE rules, ladder/fall practices, ventilation controls, spill
  response, waste handling, and incident documentation.
- [ ] Set up bookkeeping, receipt capture, mileage/equipment records, a dedicated business payment
  lane, and tax reserves.
- [ ] Prepare permission forms for before/after photography and strip faces, addresses, license
  plates, mail, and location metadata before publication.

### 5. Website and communications launch gate

Do not remove concept mode until all items below are verified together:

- [ ] Active CCB record matches the final public business identity and endorsement.
- [ ] Bond and insurance are active; any required lead-safe credential is active.
- [ ] The CCB number appears on the website and other covered advertising.
- [ ] Business name, phone, email, service area, legal entity, and pricing policy are approved.
- [ ] The domain is purchased and controlled by Andrew; inbound and outbound business email are
  tested without exposing credentials.
- [ ] Real project photos have written publication permission and privacy cleanup.
- [ ] Every contractor claim is re-audited; the footer, schema, manifest, social metadata, robots,
  sitemap, CTA, estimator, and contact flow are updated as one release.
- [ ] Static verification, keyboard/mobile checks, link tests, form tests, and a public-page review
  pass before launch.
- [ ] Andrew explicitly authorizes the public contractor launch.
